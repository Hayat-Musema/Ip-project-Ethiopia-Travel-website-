Lalibela

The New Jerusalem — North Wollo Zone, Amhara Region

Introduction

Lalibela is history frozen in stone, a place where faith and architecture merge in the most breathtaking way. Known as the 'New Jerusalem', it features 11 medieval monolithic cave churches carved from rock.

Key Highlights

  • Rock-hewn churches (St. George, St. Mary)
  • UNESCO World Heritage Site
  • Rich religious festivals and pilgrimages
