Harar

Historic Walled City — Eastern Ethiopia

Introduction

Harar is one of the world’s oldest Islamic cities and a UNESCO-listed walled town. It’s renowned for its narrow alleyways, colorful Harari houses, bustling markets, and unique traditions such as the nightly hyena feeding ceremonies.

Key Highlights

  • UNESCO World Heritage — historic walled town
  • Harari architecture and painted houses
  • Vibrant markets and local crafts
  • Traditional hyena feeding at the city edge
